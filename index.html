About Us

Building Excellence Since 2013

IRSHAD AZHAR ENTERPRISES, Established in 2013 as an individual / sole proprietor firm, has grown to become a trusted name in the industrial services sector. With offices in Lahore and Karachi, we serve clients across Pakistan with dedication and professionalism.
